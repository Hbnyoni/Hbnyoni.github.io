Work

Live platforms and software

SPECTRA Mobile Live

Personal exposure and trajectory app with a web explorer. It turns GPS tracks and wearable sensor records into time-weighted, microenvironment-resolved personal exposure. Piloted at the MSU Research & Innovation Week and at AARSE 2026, Harare (121 sessions). Open source, archived on Zenodo, with a full user training course.

App Web explorer Code Zenodo Course

GIPEX Live

Earth-observation indicator platform built in Python Dash: 57 satellite-derived indicators, including PM2.5, heat stress, greenness and flood risk, for 21 African countries.

Platform Courses

CHEAQI dashboard and data

Interactive personal PM2.5 exposure dashboard for sub-Saharan Africa, openly described research datasets, and an archived evidence map of geospatial proxy indicators for air-pollution exposure.
